Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Welcome to Compress PDF & Image Tools ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our mobile application.

Summary: All your files (PDFs, images, and documents) are processed 100% locally on your device. We do NOT upload, collect, view, or store your documents or media on any external cloud server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your sensitive data and personal privacy:

  • No Personal Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide your name, phone number, or email to use the app.
  • No File Uploads: When you compress a PDF or resize an image, all processing happens entirely offline inside your device's memory. Your files never leave your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob to support free access to all utility tools. Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage data in accordance with its privacy practices, including:

  • Advertising IDs (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Device identifiers, operating system version, and model
  • App performance metrics and crash logs
  • Coarse location data (IP address country/region level) for relevant ad delivery

For more details on how Google uses data from apps, please read Google's Advertising Policies & Privacy Terms.

3. Device Permissions Used

The app requests the following minimal Android permissions strictly for functionality:

  • Read / Write Media & Storage: Required solely to let you choose documents or photos to compress/convert and save the resulting files back to your device storage. We utilize the Android Photo Picker and standard Storage Access Framework whenever available.
  • Internet & Network State: Required exclusively for loading AdMob advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

5. Security of Your Data

Because your files are handled strictly within the local application sandbox on your device, your documents and photos cannot be accessed or intercepted by external parties.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
